## How the real footage was captured

- **Browser.** The live site in headless Chromium (Playwright) at a 1920×1080 viewport, DPR 1, with WebGL through
  ANGLE/SwiftShader.
- **Virtual clock.** SwiftShader renders only about 1–5 fps in real time, so a **virtual clock** was injected
  (`scripts/vt-inject.js`). It patches `requestAnimationFrame`, timers, `performance.now` and `Date.now`, and steps all
  CSS and Web Animations. The playback rate is frozen through CDP. The recorder (`scripts/rec-lib.js`) advances
  exactly 1/30 s and then takes a screenshot (JPEG q95). The result is smooth, real 30 fps footage of the actual app
  with no dropped or duplicated frames.
- **Cursor.** A visible cursor overlay follows real Playwright mouse events, because headless Chromium draws no cursor.
- **No fps probe trip.** The app's fps probe measured 60 fps (quality 2: full shadows) because the probe window was
  stepped at 16.7 ms. That means no "rendering slowly" toast and no automatic quality drop.
- **Scripts.** `scripts/capture.js` captured shot A. `scripts/capture2.js` captured shots B, C and D.

## Audio pipeline

1. **Narration.** `scripts/tts_final.py` runs Kokoro-82M with voice `af_heart` at speed 1.1, US English, one take per
   sentence (8 takes), and saves per-word timestamps to `audio/final_tokens.json`.
   - **Audition.** `af_heart`, `af_bella` and `af_nicole` were all synthesized (`audio/tts/audition_*.wav`).
   - **Pacing.** `af_heart` was tightest: 22.6 s of speech for the script, against 24.2 s for `af_bella`. `af_nicole`
     was breathy and slow (11.2 s for one sentence) and its ASR transcript misread "1,915".
   - **ASR check.** Transcripts of `af_heart` and `af_bella` were both word-perfect.
   - **Choice.** `af_heart` (Kokoro's top-graded voice) was chosen for clarity and pace.
   - **Spoken rendering.** "PARALLAX X" is spoken as "Parallax Ex" for reliable pronunciation; the on-screen spelling
     is unchanged.
2. **Score and SFX.** `scripts/music.py timeline.json` produces `audio/stems/music.wav` and `sfx.wav`.
   - **Chords.** Dm9 → B♭maj7 → F → C → Gm (dark, under FAILED) → B♭ → C → Asus4 (riser) → Dmaj9 (resolve).
   - **Arpeggio.** Plays 8ths during Discovery and 16ths during Expedition Lab, and drops out under FAILED.
   - **SFX.** Placed on actual click and reveal frames.
3. **Mix.** `scripts/mix.py` produces `PARALLAX_X_DEMO_NARRATION_KOKORO.wav` (raw 24 kHz Kokoro output placed on the
   timeline, unprocessed) and `PARALLAX_X_DEMO_AUDIO_MIX.wav` (48 kHz, 24-bit master).
   - **Ducking.** −13 dB.
   - **Loudness.** −15 LUFS target, true-peak limiter with −1.5 dBTP ceiling.
   - **Stems.** Kept in `audio/stems/stem_{voice,music_ducked,sfx}.wav` for later corrections.

## Rebuild

```bash
cd video-production
python3 scripts/make_timeline.py
python3 scripts/srt.py
.venv-tts/bin/python scripts/music.py timeline.json
.venv-tts/bin/python scripts/mix.py
./scripts/encode.sh
```

`encode.sh` pipes `scripts/compose.py`, a PIL compositor, into FFmpeg with these settings:

```
libx264 -preset slow -crf 15 -profile:v high -pix_fmt yuv420p -r 30 (BT.709)
aac 256k 48 kHz stereo -t 29.300 -movflags +faststart
```

**Environment.**
- FFmpeg 6.1.1; Python 3.13 for the compositor (Pillow, numpy).
- A Python 3.12 venv for Kokoro 0.9.4, scipy, pyloudnorm and faster-whisper.
- Playwright Chromium.
- Fonts: Inter / Inter Display.

**Source media not committed.** The raw captures (`src/capture/*`, ~1,100 JPEG frames) and the downloaded concept
films (`src/films`) are regenerated by the capture scripts and the film download URLs. They are not committed, to
keep the repository small.
