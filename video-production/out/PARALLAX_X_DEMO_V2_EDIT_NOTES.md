## The rover capture (E_rover)

- **Shot design.** The rover act is one continuous take. Its camera moves are the app's own Follow and Close-up
  cameras, triggered with the real UI buttons. The cursor is hidden; the clicks happen outside the cropped area.
- **Crisp framing.** The app's 3D viewport is small at 1920×1080, so the live site was rendered in a 2560×1440 window
  at DPR 1.5. Only the viewport was captured at device pixels: CDP clip `scale: 1.5`, 2132×1125 px. A 16:9 region
  (1707×960 px) maps to the frame, a modest 1.12× scale with no soft blow-up. A first take at CSS resolution
  (`src/capture/E_rover_lowres`) was used only to proof the edit.
- **Render quality.** The fps probe read 60 fps (quality 2: shadows, full materials). The rover rig loaded with
  6 split wheels.
- **Wheels and mast.** Wheel spin is the app's distance-driven wheel rotation. The mast-head scan is the app's own
  `rover.scan()` at a point of interest.
- **Not used: the mast raise.** The app also has a mast-raise "wake" (the model's rigged clip), but it only plays once
  when a scene loads under the default overview camera, where the rover is small. It was therefore not used; the
  mast-head scan is the mechanical detail shown.
- **Disclaimers.** The app's own on-screen chip ("Illustrative terrain-following visualization · not a validated
  mobility simulation · HiRISE Mars scene · rover enlarged ×41") was cropped out. A larger, readable notice replaces
  it: "ILLUSTRATIVE HiRISE TERRAIN TRAVERSE · NOT MOBILITY PHYSICS".

**Script:** `scripts/capture_rover.js`

## Narration

- **Engine.** Kokoro-82M, voice `af_heart`, US English, speed 1.1, 9 separate takes in `audio/v2/af_heart/`.
- **Audition.** `af_bella` was also generated (`audio/v2/af_bella/`). Timing is similar (`af_bella` leaves ~0.1 s
  longer lead-in silence).
- **Choice.** `af_heart` was kept for consistency with V1 and because it is Kokoro's top-graded voice.
- **Listening.** **Not auditioned by ear.** This cloud session has no audio output, so the final judgement of tone
  needs a human listen. The ASR transcript of the final mix is word-perfect.
